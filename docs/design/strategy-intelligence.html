A strategy may request a size; the engine caps it and only ever shrinks it. Any change that loosens a limit is refused, and no research output can grant live trading.

What this screen will not do

Copy rules (these are the product)

  • Never call a delayed or simulated feed live. The word appears on the legend, the card, the row and the JSON field.
  • NO TRADE is a result. It always names the gate and the number that failed it.
  • Units travel with numbers: R, %, currency, bars, folds.
  • An unreachable feed reports its error, never a stale price.
  • Disabled actions stay visible with the reason beside them: approvals are CLI-only.
